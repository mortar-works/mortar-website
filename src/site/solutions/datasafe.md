DataSafe provides governed, secure analytical infrastructure for organisations that need to work with sensitive, complex, or regulated data. Whether deployed within your existing systems or hosted and managed by us, DataSafe creates a controlled environment where data can be ingested, linked, and analysed safely — with full audit trails, role-based access controls, and compliance configurations built to your context. It is the foundation that makes everything else possible: the secure base from which modelling, research, and intelligence work can proceed without compromising data protection obligations.
