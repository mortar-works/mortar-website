Forma delivers custom population intelligence, service design capability, and digital twin infrastructure for organisations that need to understand their population deeply and test their decisions before making them. Built around your data, your geography, and your specific design problems, Forma combines behavioural modelling and scenario simulation to give you a computational picture of the population you serve and the services you run. The result is design intelligence: the evidence and analytical capability to make better commissioning decisions, plan more effective services, and understand the likely impact of change before it happens.
